About the Founder

The story, background, and vision behind Lucky Ish Smart Learning System.

Manuel Wandera • Lucky Ish Programmer

Manuel Wandera—popularly known online as Lucky Ish Programmer on Facebook and lucky is 254 on Instagram—is a young Kenyan student developer and educational creator from Rumbiye, Agenga/Naguba Sub-location, Busia County, Kenya.

He is a Grade 10 learner at Bukiri Senior Secondary School, dedicated to building practical digital education tools that give fellow students easy access to full-term revision notes, structured practice questions, and end-of-topic quizzes across Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Grade 10, Form 3, and Form 4.

Driven by a passion for coding and solving real classroom challenges, Manuel created Lucky Ish Smart Learning System as an independent student-led project to help learners revise smarter in Busia County, across Kenya, and beyond.
